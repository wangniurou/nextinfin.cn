Hyaluronic-Acid de alcohol

TypeBeverage
Spec50ml / bottle in a box
Key Benefits
  • Full-molecular-weight sodium hyaluronate GAUSSIAN-HA forms a protective film in the stomach, encapsulating alcohol, delaying its absorption and mitigating irritation.
  • Reduces blood alcohol concentration and eases the burden on the liver.
  • Activates the activities of alcohol dehydrogenase (ADH) and acetaldehyde dehydrogenase (ALDH) to accelerate alcohol degradation.
  • Residual alcohol can also be "second-encapsulated" for metabolic excretion, preventing repeated re-absorption.

Diacylglycerol Series

1 Product

Diacylglycerol Oil with 20/40/60/80/85% DAG Content

TypeFunctional edible oil
DosageSuggested daily intake 15–25 grams
  • Distinguished by its unique molecular structure — unlike conventional edible oils rich in triacylglycerol, DAG oil contains diacylglycerol as its major component.
  • When ingested, DAG is metabolized in a different pathway inside the human body. It is less likely to be stored as body fat, and tends to be burned for energy instead.
  • Can be used as a full replacement for ordinary cooking oil for stir-frying, cold salads, simmering and daily home cooking.
  • Long-term continuous intake helps support healthy fat management, blood lipid profiles and stable blood glucose levels.

About Us

Building the industrial translation pipeline for health products

NextInfin HealthTech Co., Ltd. was founded by Megalife Health Technology Group Co., Ltd., a wholly-owned subsidiary of Zhejiang United Science & Technology Co., Ltd. (Stock Code: 000925), and officially launched operations on June 1, 2025.

We cooperate with the Key Laboratory of Tibetan Medicine Research (CAS). Our team comprises over 30 leading experts including academicians, professors, doctoral supervisors, postdocs and PhDs, with strong strengths in R&D and active ingredient extraction & formulation.

Partnered with Shanghai Xinhetai Biotechnology Co., Ltd., we leverage synthetic biology as core technology. Targeting the trillion-dollar market of preventive medicine and medicinal & edible ingredients, we pursue the modernization of traditional Chinese medicine in the health tech sector.

We significantly boost bioavailability, purity and product performance, solving traditional bottlenecks of low extraction yield and inconsistent quality. A full industrial translation pipeline from lab to manufacturing has been established, offering replicable technical models for mass production of health products.
